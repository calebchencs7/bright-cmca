Communication job
By the end, a mixed research audience should be able to reproduce a safe, evidence-driven AI research workflow because one real project is unpacked from observation to paper.

Audience and tone
- Chinese-speaking researchers and students; assume they know research basics but not advanced AI prompting.
- Practical, candid, non-promotional. AI accelerates the loop; the researcher owns claims, evidence, and decisions.

Narrative arc (about 30 minutes)
1. Open: AI changes iteration speed, not scientific accountability.
2. Establish the case: multimodal building damage assessment with BRIGHT.
3. Idea case: observation -> constrained prompt -> hypothesis tree -> falsifiable design -> human screening.
4. Literature case: question matrix -> search strings -> evidence cards -> gap synthesis.
5. Coding and experiments: smallest interface patch -> 2x2 ablation -> multi-backbone/event checks.
6. Visualization: decide the sentence before the chart; show both aggregate and event-level behavior.
7. Writing: claim-evidence-warrant, paragraph rewrite, and claim audit.
8. Close: reusable prompt contract and a concrete next action.

Visual system
- Codex Grid: white canvas, black type, pale gray evidence surfaces, blue accent.
- Use PingFang SC for Chinese; scientific figures stay in their original color.
- Alternate sparse message, two-column explanation, full-width evidence, timeline, and chart silhouettes.
- No tool-logo collage; tools are named only as replaceable examples.

Case evidence
- BRIGHT overview and framework figures.
- CMCA, DA-CutMix, and technology-roadmap figures.
- Actual implementation excerpts from UNetCMCA.py and make_data_loader.py.
- Ablation results and per-event robustness discussion from latex/main.tex and the workbook renders.
